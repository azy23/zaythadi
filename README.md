
# ဈေးသတိ (Zaythadi)

**မြန်မာ့ဈေးနှုန်း စောင့်ကြည့်ရေးနှင့် စီးပွားရေး အန္တရာယ် ကြိုတင်သတိပေးစနစ်**

Zaythadi is a community-based platform prototype for collecting local market prices and monitoring market conditions in Myanmar.

## Features

- Submit product prices by township
- Record product units, dates and sources
- Search and review saved price reports
- Export price reports as CSV
- Submit market news and unverified reports
- Store data in the browser
- Basic offline access through a service worker
- Responsive design for phones and computers

## Important limitations

- This prototype stores reports in the current browser only.
- Reports are not automatically shared with other users.
- Market news and user-submitted prices are not independently verified.
- A shared database, moderation and reliable early-warning analysis are future improvements.

## Privacy and safety

Do not submit personal information, phone numbers, private identities or information that could put a source at risk.
